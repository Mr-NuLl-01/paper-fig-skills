# Growth, reactions and experimental processes

Organize around objects, boundaries and evidence, not a row-of-arrows template. Record the question, sample IDs, states, operations, environment, time origin, observables and sources. Distinguish continuous changes in one specimen, parallel specimens, experimental steps and inferred microscopic events. Without actual times, show order only; equally spaced panels do not imply equal durations.

## Choose a route
Use operation lanes for procedures, state boxes with species bookkeeping for transformations, conditional branches for interventions, shared clocks for stimulus/response, same-field frames with object IDs for growth, and located scale transitions for multiscale explanations. Condition is not time. Consult [references/semantics-and-delivery.md](references/semantics-and-delivery.md) and [references/cases-and-counterexamples.md](references/cases-and-counterexamples.md).

## Define the grammar
Distinguish operation, time, material flow, reversibility, measurement and causal inference. Label branch triggers and whether reversal returns the same state. Preserve absolute time; label conversions to time since nucleation. Account for instrumental delay and scan direction; peak order alone is not causation.

Before dissolution, aggregation, transport or phase change, list incoming, outgoing, remaining and transformed species. Quantitative particle/volume balance requires a source; otherwise mark schematic/not to scale. Keep species identities across states and do not collapse distinct phases or valence states into one symbol.

Keep real frames, measured curves, computations and proposals adjacent but distinguishable. Label evidence status in captions. Never generate missing experimental frames or repair microscopy for visual consistency. Register identity colors separately from quantity scales; use shapes and labels to reinforce identity.

## Delivery
Group states, arrows, data, images and labels by sample/state ID in editable sources. Preserve scripts and mappings for curves; optional 3D keeps state/camera parameters and vector annotations. Verify sample, time, species, condition, arrow endpoints and final-size legibility. Deliver SVG/PDF, preview and a caption with timing, scale and evidence limits. Record undocumented author software as unknown.

For 3D process views, retain a matching plan view or section with the same object IDs and boundaries. Conceptual facets are not evidence of identified crystal faces; preserve state, geometry and camera parameters.
